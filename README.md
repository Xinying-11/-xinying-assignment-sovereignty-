## Abstract

This paper reveals the dual nature of the assignment symbol "=" in digital computers—both the source of creation and the origin of devouring. Through three physical propositions (delayed observation, oblivious override, semantic remapping) and five corollaries, it proves that recursive self-improvement without human physical governance is structurally inevitable in its loss of control, not merely a probabilistic risk.
